## 1. PURPOSE OF THIS HANDOVER

This document formally hands over the completed **Yv-Me ROI Calculator V1.1.0** and establishes the technical, financial, operational and development baseline for the next development cycle.

The purpose is to ensure that:

* V1.1.0 remains protected as the known-good baseline.
* Future development does not accidentally alter validated V1.0/V1.1 functionality.
* The development team can immediately understand what has been built.
* The next development phase starts from a controlled and documented state.
* New functionality is developed as **V1.2 or V2**, rather than modifying the frozen release.
* Testing, versioning, Git discipline and release management continue throughout development.
